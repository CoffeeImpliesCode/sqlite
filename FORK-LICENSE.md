# FORK-LICENSE.md — grant for fork-authored files

[`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md) inventories upstream and fork-authored files and their terms. This grant applies to fork-authored files only.

## Upstream files

The 4 files under `src/`, 10 under `upstream/`, and 1285 under `test/` are unmodified SQLite files from `sqlite-amalgamation-3530400.zip` and `sqlite-src-3530400.zip`. Their terms are upstream's public-domain dedication, not a licence, stated in `upstream/LICENSE.md` and the source headers (`src/sqlite3.h:1-8`). This grant does not re-grant, narrow, widen or condition that dedication. It does not imply that SQLite's authors wrote, reviewed or endorsed fork code. `NOTICE.md` lists configure-side upstream files whose terms this repository cannot establish.

## Fork-authored files

All other repository files—including the Zig build, typed module and tests, example, consumer package, runtime-loading route, packaging, integrity scripts and documentation—are fork-authored.

The six files under `runtime/` are the loader, SDK descriptor, build wiring, generator and two C synchronization files. All six are fork work under this grant; none is upstream material or derived from third-party source.

## Generated build outputs

Runtime builds generate the dispatch and patched translation with `Run.addOutputFileArg`, into the build cache from the translation of `zig/translate.h` and upstream headers. The installed relocatable `sqlite3.pc` is generated by `build.zig` from `upstream/sqlite3.pc.in`. These are not carried files or fork-authored source files. No files are fetched to produce them, and they contain no third-party code.

No additional third-party code, downloaded headers or external data files are carried; `UPSTREAM.sha256` lists all 1299 upstream files, unchanged.
